## 🧱 Indonesia-Specific Blocks Added to Database

### 1. **WhatsApp Business Block**
- Type: `whatsapp-business`
- Category: Indonesia
- Features: FAB mode, Indonesia phone formatting (08xxx → +628xxx), pulse animation, expand on hover
- Properties: phoneNumber, message, buttonStyle, fabPosition, fabSize, showLabel, enablePulse, expandOnHover

### 2. **Delivery Platform Block**
- Type: `delivery-platform`
- Category: Indonesia
- Features: GoFood, GrabFood, ShopeeFood integration, ratings, badges, promos
- Layouts: buttons, grid, carousel

### 3. **Marketplace Block**
- Type: `marketplace`
- Category: Indonesia
- Features: Tokopedia, Shopee, TikTok Shop, store badges, ratings, featured products
- Layouts: buttons, grid, mixed

### 4. **Location Block**
- Type: `location`
- Category: Indonesia
- Features: Google Maps embed, opening hours, Buka/Tutup status, directions button
- Properties: googleMapsUrl, address, phone, openingHours, showCurrentStatus

### 5. **QRIS Payment Block**
- Type: `qris-payment`
- Category: Indonesia
- Features: QRIS QR code, e-wallet logos (Gopay, OVO, Dana, ShopeePay), preset amounts
- Properties: qrisImage, merchantName, paymentMethods, presetAmounts, instructions
