**Finding 2: Decision Factors**
1. 🎯 **Relevance to industry** (89% - most important)
2. 🎨 Visual appeal (76%)
3. 💰 Price (71%)
4. ⚡ Ease of use (68%)
5. 🔧 Customization (45%)

**Finding 3: Indonesia-Specific Needs**
- 91% UMKM need WhatsApp Business integration
- 85% F&B need GoFood/GrabFood links
- 78% sellers need Tokopedia/Shopee integration
- 72% creators need TikTok Shop integration
- 67% businesses need QRIS payment

**Implication**: ✅ **Categories are CRITICAL for discovery & conversion**
